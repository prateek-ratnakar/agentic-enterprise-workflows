Consumer Goods and Retail | Order to Cash | Collections > Deduction management > Dispute resolution

A retailer short-pays an invoice and cites a reason. The workflow decides whether the deduction is valid (issue credit) or invalid (dispute and rebill), and routes judgment calls to an analyst.

Synthetic data. 40 generated cases; the human reviewer is simulated from ground truth the way a UAT reference set would be. Value figures use the labelled assumptions below, not client data.
