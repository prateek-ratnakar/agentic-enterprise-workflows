Banking and Financial Services | Lending lifecycle (credit to recovery) | Collections > Early delinquency (1-30 days past due) > Customer response handling

A borrower in early delinquency responds to a reminder. The workflow decides the next action: book a promise to pay, stop collections, reverse a charge, or hand hardship and flagged cases to a trained officer.

Synthetic data. 40 generated cases; the human reviewer is simulated from ground truth the way a UAT reference set would be. Value figures use the labelled assumptions below, not client data.
