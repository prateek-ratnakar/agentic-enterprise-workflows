Cross-industry (Manufacturing, High-Tech, Retail) | Source to Pay | Accounts payable > Three-way match > Exception resolution

A supplier invoice fails the three-way match against purchase order and goods receipt. The workflow decides whether to release payment, hold it, or send the exception to the buyer or budget owner.
